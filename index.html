UNDER THE BONNET

A catalogue, with provenance.

Vehicle names, manufacturers, classes and model IDs come from the game-data catalogue. Separate models remain separate. Drift tune packages are grouped with their base model rather than counted as a different car.

Every new collection starts with no owned vehicles, watchlist entries or garages. Add vehicles to Owned or Watchlist, create garages with your own themes, and track each move. Models without a legitimate permanent storage route are listed as Not obtainable.

Website unavailable does not always mean LS Car Meet required. Delisted vehicles may return at weekly dealers. Benny’s vehicles require a donor conversion. Some special vehicles are free spawns, career rewards or mission-only models.

Watchlist purchase routes and the 31-model HSW roster were cross-checked against GTACars. Acquisition routes are sourced separately from performance data. Non-ownable NPC and mission variants are clearly separated. HSW eligibility does not mean you have installed HSW. No live discounts are implied.

Stock images use unmodified vehicle-reference renders from Cfx.re/FiveM, supplemented with FiveForge stock game-file renders for newer models and GTA Wiki stock reference images for Enhanced models. A missing image stays labelled rather than substituting a different or customized car.
